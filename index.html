AnasSMP Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains what information AnasSMP Bot (the bot) collects, how it is used, and how you can ask for it to be removed. The bot is operated by Anasnoor.

Information the bot collects

  • Message counts. When you send a message in a server where the bot is present, the bot adds one to a count tied to your Discord user ID and that server's ID. The bot does not save the content of your messages.
  • Warnings. When a moderator uses the warn command on you, the bot saves your Discord user ID and the reason the moderator typed.
  • Command inputs. When you use a command, the bot receives what you typed for it, such as a member, a reason, a duration, or the text for the say command. The bot uses this only to carry out the command and does not save it, apart from warning reasons described above.
  • Direct messages. If you send the bot a direct message, it replies with a fixed help message. It does not save the content of your direct messages.
  • Public Discord information. Commands such as userinfo and avatar show public details like a username, account creation date, server join date, and profile picture. The bot reads these from Discord when you run the command and does not save them.

How the information is used

Message counts are used for the stats and leaderboard commands. Warnings are used so moderators can keep track of warnings given in their server. The information is not used for advertising or profiling.

Sharing

The bot does not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. Stats and leaderboard results are shown only in the server where the command is used. When a moderator uses the bot to kick, ban, mute, or change a nickname, Discord records that action in the server's audit log under Discord's own policies.

Storage and retention

The bot stores message counts and warnings in files on a device operated by the bot owner. This information is kept until it is deleted. Counts and warnings are kept separately for each server.

Your choices

You can ask for your message count or warnings to be deleted by contacting the bot owner using the details below. Removing the bot from a server stops new counting there. Information already stored for that server will be deleted if you ask.

Children

The bot is meant for people who meet Discord's minimum age requirement, which is at least 13 years old, or higher in some countries. It is not intended for younger children.

Discord

Your use of Discord is covered by Discord's Terms of Service and Discord's Privacy Policy.

Using the bot

When using /nick, you shall not name the bot anything inappropriate.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the bot's features change. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last updated.
